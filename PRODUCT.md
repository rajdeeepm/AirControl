# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: people whose hands hurt — RSI, carpal tunnel, limited mobility — who
need a third input modality that works every day at their desk. They tolerate
setup effort but have zero tolerance for false actions. Secondary: tech
enthusiasts drawn by the Jarvis fantasy, who stay only if the tool keeps
earning its place. The person configuring the app is the person using it; no
surface-vs-user split.

## Product Purpose

AirControl turns a laptop webcam into a reliable gesture-control channel for
the desktop: coarse, high-confidence actions (media, mute, scroll, window
switching) triggered by deliberate hand gestures, processed entirely
on-device. Success is a user who still has it running a month later — near-zero
false positives per hour with high recall on deliberate gestures.

## Positioning

The first gesture control engineered to clinical-grade reliability: when
uncertain, it does nothing. Every screen reinforces that the system is honest,
measured, and under the user's control.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, trustworthy. The interface behaves like a good instrument: it
reports numbers honestly (confidence scores, false-fire counts), stays silent
when idle, and never dramatizes. The Jarvis dream is the horizon; the product
voice is the flight computer, not the hologram.

## Anti-references

Not sci-fi cosplay: no neon HUD grids, glowing wireframe hands, or
"futuristic" glassmorphism. Not demo-ware: no confetti, no gamified streaks.
Not a SaaS marketing dashboard: no hero metrics with gradient accents, no
identical card grids. Nothing that hides or prettifies a misfire.

## Design Principles

1. **The HUD never lies.** Every fired action is shown with its real
   confidence; abstentions are visible on demand; numbers are never rounded
   into reassurance.
2. **Silence when idle.** The interface recedes when nothing is happening;
   attention is spent only on state changes the user caused.
3. **Model restraint.** Five to seven mappings is the ceiling users retain;
   the UI never encourages hoarding gestures.
4. **Privacy is shown, not claimed.** Screens display what is stored (landmark
   skeletons, never video) and make deletion a first-class, truthful action.
5. **Recovery is cheap.** Undo, re-record, recalibrate are always one obvious
   step away; errors are treated as expected events, not exceptions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The primary audience has motor-strain conditions: large hit targets, full
keyboard operability, no interaction that requires precise or sustained
pointing. Respect prefers-reduced-motion throughout (skeleton animations pause
to a still frame). WCAG 2.1 AA contrast minimums; state never conveyed by
color alone.
